Trust and transparency

Good matches start
with honest data.

HyperMatch is a demo marketplace. Public profile discovery is distinct from metrics that need verification.

Balanced matching model

Initial proposed weights:

  • Niche and content relevance — 30%
  • Telugu audience and location fit — 20%
  • Platform and format — 15%
  • Budget compatibility — 15%
  • Available performance evidence — 10%
  • Language and preferences — 10%

Unknown fields should not automatically count against a creator. Scores are relevance estimates, not guaranteed campaign success.

Data standards

  • Public profile links are provided for discovery; verify original profiles before outreach.
  • Follower counts, demographics, engagement and fees are not presented as verified live metrics.
  • State association and niche should be confirmed before contracting.
  • A directory listing is not an endorsement, verification or availability promise.
  • Contact creators through public profiles or official business channels.
